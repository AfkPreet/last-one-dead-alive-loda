HOW TO DIE PROPERLY

Twelve lamps in a dark field. The oil in yours is not yours — somebody poured it in and went out. The night ends when the last lamp does, and only that lamp is still burning when it ends.
Get what you were handed to the end of the night.

Run into the spikes.
They are not hazards. They are the only fuel left.
Flame is time, at a worse and worse rate.
Your first 10 flame buy about 7 seconds. Your tenth 10 buy about 2. The brighter you burn, the more you pay per second alive — watch the rate climb as you eat.
The dim eat the bright.
Touch a brighter lamp and you tear the oil straight out of it. A third of it spills and never burns at all — you can win the night and still leave the field darker. Being the biggest fire here makes you food.
The light is closing.
Outside it the dark strips your oil away. Speed comes from oil, so don't get caught slow and far out.
Tap to dash.
It costs flame. You are spending life to move — that is the deal here.
You cannot win without going out.
The night ends when the last lamp does. Make sure that lamp is you. There is a LET GO button in the corner that ends you instantly, no confirmation. It is not a trick — the whole point is that you could set this down at any moment and don't.
